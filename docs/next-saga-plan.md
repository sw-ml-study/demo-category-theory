# Saga plan: duality, universal constructions, and Yoneda

This saga implements CT020-CT044 from `docs/concept-inventory.md`. It extends
the established finite, executable teaching style and keeps capability-first
ordering. It does not require general polymorphic category syntax.

## Steps

1. **Inventory and capability matrix** — map all 25 IDs to prerequisites;
   probe finite-set representation of arrows, opposite lookup, empty/singleton
   domains, commuting cones, and function tables. Update every documentation
   ledger and record precise upstream asks only for measured blockers.
2. **Duality and universal endpoints** — demonstrate opposite categories,
   dual statements, initial objects, terminal objects, and zero-object
   distinction with finite functions and unique-arrow witnesses.
3. **Equalizers and coequalizers** — compute the largest agreeing subobject
   and the quotient generated by parallel arrows; verify universal
   factorization and show failing mediators.
4. **Pullbacks and pushouts** — implement database-join/fiber-product and
   tagged-merge examples; verify their universal properties visually.
5. **Cones, cocones, finite limits, and finite colimits** — unify the prior
   constructions as diagram-shaped universal objects and state the exact
   finite boundary around completeness/cocompleteness.
6. **Representables and hom-functors** — use finite function tables to execute
   covariant/contravariant hom action, representable/corepresentable examples,
   universal elements, and naturality fixtures.
7. **Yoneda and category of elements** — execute a finite Yoneda
   correspondence in both directions, verify round-trip/naturality, visualize
   the category of elements, and introduce density only to the level supported
   by the fixture.
8. **Integration and handoff** — audit CT020-CT044 coverage, all visuals and
   documentation, rebuild artifacts, run the full gate, and queue the next
   inventory saga based on measured results.

Every step runs `just check`, creates a detailed work commit, completes and
commits Agentrail metadata, pushes both commits, and reports the next step and
blockers. Unsupported concepts remain tracked by stable ID rather than being
silently omitted.
