## Measured findings

- `range(0)` is a usable empty finite domain; `[0]` is a usable singleton.
- Numeric arrays are honest finite function tables, and nested numeric arrays
  can enumerate a bounded family of such functions.
- Transpose supplies a direct representation of reversing a finite relation.
- Pointwise indexing and nested equality are sufficient for cone commutativity.
- The primary friction is not execution but abstraction: arbitrary categories,
  functors, natural transformations, quotients, and all-small-diagram claims
  require explicit finite encodings.

No new upstream ask is justified yet. The next lessons should extract small
named helpers only after repetition appears. CT035-CT036 remain explanatory
boundaries, and CT044 remains conditional on the Yoneda fixture.
